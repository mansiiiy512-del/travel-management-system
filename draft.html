📍 Popular Travel Places

Agra

Famous for the Taj Mahal and Mughal architecture.

📍 Places to Visit

Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Mehtab Bagh and Itmad-ud-Daulah's Tomb.

🍴 Famous Food

Petha, Mughlai food, Bedai and Jalebi.

🏔️ Adventure

Heritage walks, photography tours and local sightseeing.

⭐ Special

Historical monuments and Mughal architecture.

Manali

Beautiful Himalayan destination famous for mountains and adventure.

📍 Places to Visit

Solang Valley, Hadimba Temple, Old Manali, Mall Road and nearby mountain areas.

🍴 Famous Food

Siddu, Momos, Thukpa and Himachali food.

🏔️ Adventure

Trekking, river rafting, paragliding, skiing in suitable seasons and camping.

⭐ Special

Mountains, valleys, rivers and beautiful scenery.

Goa

Famous for beaches, tourism and coastal scenery.

📍 Places to Visit

Baga Beach, Calangute Beach, Fort Aguada, Panjim and Dudhsagar Falls.

🍴 Famous Food

Goan fish curry, rice, seafood dishes and Bebinca.

🏔️ Adventure

Water sports, kayaking, scuba diving, parasailing and boat trips.

⭐ Special

Beaches, coastal scenery and local culture.

Jaipur

Famous for forts, palaces, culture and history.

📍 Places to Visit

Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar and Nahargarh Fort.

🍴 Famous Food

Dal Baati Churma, Ghewar, Pyaaz Kachori and Rajasthani food.

🏔️ Adventure

Ziplining, cycling tours, heritage walks and outdoor activities.

⭐ Special

Royal palaces, forts, colourful markets and Rajasthani culture.

🥾 Trekking

🚣 River Rafting

⛺ Camping
